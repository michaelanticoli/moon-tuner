The Lunar Chaperone · MMXXVI

The Lunar Chaperone is a year-long digital workbook by Moontuner. It follows the moon through twenty-six half-cycles — every New Moon to Full Moon, every Full Moon to New Moon — and gives you a spread for each one.

Each spread has three parts. An Intention to plant into the lunation. A fourteen-day Arc — a directive a day, in order, worked in your own hand. A Reflection to close it. Nothing here is a forecast; this is a book you write.

Moontuner is a small design house tuning secular tools to the rhythms of the sky. We make workbooks, calendars, and quiet software for people who want to keep time with something older than the clock.

The Lunar Chaperone is a workbook program that follows the framework of the Phasecraft curriculum which is core to the Moontuner Method
