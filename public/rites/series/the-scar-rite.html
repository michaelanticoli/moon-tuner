Seven stations. The odd number is deliberate — no neat symmetry, no clean balance. Scars don't resolve into tidy arcs. They leave a mark, and the mark changes the architecture around it.

Someone said something once. It might have been one sentence, one afternoon, one offhand remark they forgot before the end of the week. But you didn't forget. You built a policy around it — a whole behavioral architecture designed to make sure you never again earned that particular verdict. The architecture is still running. This rite audits it.

The aim isn't to erase the scar. It's to check whether the authority you gave to the person who made it was ever warranted — and whether it's time to revoke it.
